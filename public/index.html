(*) folding floor lamp

Four rod legs and three crossed tiers, all held by one kind of clamp — the same one the shoe rack uses. The bulb sits right in the middle; the shade is a band of fabric or paper in any pattern, held on by magnets. Lift the shade off, loosen the three centre clamps, and the two frames swing flat like scissors.

Shoe rack

(*) made for your doorway

A shoe rack should fit the doorway it stands in. Take one photo of your entryway: the AI finds the door, the walls and the furniture, works out the size and places a rack right into the picture. Width, tiers, shelves and rod finish can all be changed. Every design comes with a full parts list — aluminium rods and connectors, no drilling, no glue.
